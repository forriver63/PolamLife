# A first refit at MCP Central

Allow about four minutes. Start with a fresh local world. The language switch is available in the header and in dialogs. The sequence below uses the English button labels.

| Pitch beat | What to do | What the audience can see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 · Enter Po Lam | On the district map, choose **Enter Po Lam**. | Glaze Lane, a fictional donut shop, is the player’s first stop in a recognizable Hong Kong neighborhood. |
| 0:30 · Find your crew | Rank **Bubble Tea Shop**, **Gaming Store**, then **Café**. Join **The Trendsetters**. | The recommendation follows the ranked picks. The territory layer shows the crew’s starting unit at Pixel Port. |
| 1:00 · Explore the mall | Select **MCP Central → Enter the mall**. Visit Glaze Lane at Unit 01 and open its scout report. | The storefront and report identify the owner before the challenge. |
| 1:30 · Take the counter | Launch a challenge, invite both allies and play Shop Rush. Read each ticket and match five products before time expires. | Mina can catch one wrong order. Kit adds four seconds. A successful shift and three crew members capture the shop. |
| 2:00 · Show the gain | Choose **View territory change**. Select the newly marked Unit 01 or use **Return to Unit 01**. | The same unit changes from the Arcade Guild to The Trendsetters. The crew now holds two of six shops. |
| 2:20 · Decide together | Open the alliance ballot, vote for **Bubble Tea Shop** and wait for Mina and Kit. Choose **Count the votes**. | Mina chooses tea and Kit chooses gaming. With the player’s vote, tea wins 2–1. Renovation keeps the shutters down until Day 02. |
| 3:00 · See the result | Choose **Advance to next day**. Inspect Pearl Club, the mall frontage and the district map. Open **Daily**. | The new shop occupies the same unit. The paper shows the old and new storefronts together. |
| 3:40 · Ask a research question | Open **Insights** and compare the ranked picks, ballot and unit visits. | These are one local player’s selections and simulated crew votes. A resident survey could test the resulting merchant question. |

## Alternate routes

If the crew loses the rush, close the report and launch a fresh challenge from the scout report. At a friendly shop, choose **Help manage** to run a shift, staff the unit or hang pennants. For Service Relay, watch the station signals and repeat the sequence.

To demonstrate the concept’s sushi-to-hot-pot example, start a fresh game, rank **Hot Pot Restaurant**, **Convenience Store** and **Bubble Tea Shop**, and join **The Neighborhood Collective**. Challenge Roll Call at Unit 02. After capture, vote for **Hot Pot Restaurant**. Yan also favors hot pot and Lok favors the convenience store. On opening day, Little Steam takes over the sushi unit.

## Director and recovery

**Settings → Demo Director** provides the guided restart, simulated invitations, renovation clock and insights. The usual eligibility, vote and opening-day rules still apply. **Reset demo** asks for confirmation.

Refreshing restores the world and its completed actions. Pending invitations resume and allies finish voting once the player has voted. To retry an interrupted timed shift, return to the counter and open the activity again. To show a different language during a shift, use the language control inside its dialog.
